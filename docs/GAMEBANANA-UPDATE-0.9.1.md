## 1. Update: title and text

**Update name**

```
0.9.1 - D-pad right long press, free item selector, Triggers preset, Rekarina prompts
```

**Update description**

```
0.9.1 makes the D-pad right a button for its long press (Armory, item selector...), lets the item selector sit next to
Item 2, adds the Triggers control preset, brings back the sprint speed in the app and fixes merged mods and the
PlayStation prompts on Rekarina.

IMPORTANT - REBUILD ONCE
The module changed: reinstall the profile (emulator) or rebuild the game once with 0.9.1 (Longshot / Hookshot / ReLink / Rekarina).

CONTROLS
- D-pad right, long press: a short press still calls Navi; holding it does the role you give it (Armory, Item selector,
  Jump, Sword, Center camera, Sprint or Shield).
- The item selector goes on any button but the sticks and no longer takes Item 2 away.
- Triggers preset (items on the triggers, selector on B, shield on L) used by Recommended, Super Hero, Ultra Hero and
  Beta All. The profiles put the Armory on the held D-pad right. Sprint x1.25 everywhere.

APP
- Sprint speed is back in the Gameplay page. New drop-down lists. Recompile and Reinstall and recompile buttons.

REKARINA
- A merged mod's ExeFS patches no longer drop our hooks. The PlayStation / Xbox prompts, the ocarina's note buttons
  included, switch again.
```
