# GameBanana update 0.9.3

Same procedure as 0.9.1 (`references/gamebanana-update.md` of the project skill): edit the submission, attach the
presets zip (no executables), add the update post.

## 1. Update: title and text

**Update name**

```
0.9.3 - Hold roles on A / B / X / Y, Back role, sprint and jar fixes, PlayStation prompts
```

**Update description**

```
0.9.3 gives A, B, X and Y a second action when you hold them (no delay on the short press), adds the Back role (the
game's own B), makes the toggled sprint let go when you stop and stop throwing the objects you lift, fixes the manual
shield throwing lifted jars, installs the PlayStation prompts in the whole game and counts the chests that hold a
heart piece.

IMPORTANT - REBUILD ONCE
The module changed: reinstall the profile (emulator) or rebuild the game once with 0.9.3 (Longshot / Hookshot / ReLink / Rekarina).

CONTROLS
- Tap and hold on A, B, X and Y: the short press acts at once; holding it (about a third of a second) does its hold
  role (Jump, Sword, Action, Camera, Sprint, Shield, Selector, Armory or Back).
- Back: the game's own B (let go of a ledge, cancel ...), without using the B item. The Triggers and Action at the
  bottom tables, and the profiles built on them, put it on the held B.

SPRINT (TOGGLE)
- It lets go when you stop and it no longer throws the jar you lift.

FIXES
- The manual shield threw lifted jars by itself.
- The PlayStation layout now installs the font of the button glyphs too (before only the menu's picture changed).
- Heart pieces in chests count in the area counter.
- The app opens with the stamina off.
```

**Changelog entries (Bulk Add, one per line, no commas)**

```
Tap and hold on A B X and Y with no delay on the short press
New Back role: the game's own B on any button or in a hold
Triggers and Action at the bottom put Back on the held B
Toggled sprint lets go when you stop and no longer throws lifted objects
Manual shield no longer throws lifted jars by itself
PlayStation layout installs the button font in the whole game
Heart pieces in chests count in the area counter
```
